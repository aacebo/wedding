Eight centuries on a Tuscan hill.

From a medieval watchtower to a painter's villa beloved by poets—a few of the stories Striano has gathered.

13th century · A watchtower

Striano begins as a guarding tower of the powerful Ubaldini family, keeping watch over the Mugello valley north of Florence.

18th century · Villa & naturalist

The Franceschi family transforms the stronghold into a villa. It also belonged to Antonio Cocchi—physician, writer, and curator of Tuscany's museums.

1872 · A painter's home

Portraitist Michele Gordigiani buys Striano and reimagines it with architect Del Moro under the Renaissance influence of Buontalenti.

Belle Époque · Music & muses

Gordigiani's daughter Giulietta welcomes poet Gabriele D'Annunzio and actress Eleonora Duse for long summer days at the villa.

Today · A historic residence

Now a recognized Historic Residence, Striano opens its halls, gardens, and Tuscan views to host celebrations—including ours.
