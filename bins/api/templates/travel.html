Getting there

Florence Airport (FLR) is about a 40-minute drive. Pisa (PSA) is roughly 1.5 hours away, and Bologna (BLQ) is another option.

Parking

Complimentary on-site parking is available at Villa di Striano.

Local transit

Regional trains reach Borgo San Lorenzo, but a car is recommended for the villa and countryside.
